# NEU474: Stats Lab – The Pit Viper Umwelt & Capstone Organism

*This directory contains the materials and instructions for the NEU474 Stats Lab, focusing on correcting AI mammalian bias and applying agentic AI to non-traditional biological datasets.*

---

## 🐍 Task 1: The Pit Viper Umwelt Test
This task involves evaluating the sensory "Umwelt" of pit vipers using specialized thermal strike data.

* **Instruction File:** [Digital_Lab-Part_1.md](https://github.com/djtobiansky/StatsLab/blob/main/NEU474/Digital_Lab-Part_1.md)
* **Data Source:** [PitViper_Thermal_Strikes.csv](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/djtobiansky/StatsLab/refs/heads/main/NEU474/PitViper_Thermal_Strikes.csv)
* **Objective:** Load the thermal strike data into Positron and follow P.I. prompts to account for non-mammalian biological variables.
* **Deliverable:** Completed Part 1 Markdown file (exported as PDF/Word).

---

## 🧬 Task 2: Capstone Organism (Independent Study)
An independent application of Agentic AI to a self-selected non-traditional organism to create a **Draft Technical Appendix**.

### Requirements
* **Dataset Provenance:** Provide a link to an open-source dataset relevant to your chosen organism.
* **AI-Generated Code:** Use AI to write a well-annotated script for data loading, summarization, and plotting.
* **Biological Sanity Check:** One paragraph explaining the unique aspect of your organism's Umwelt that the AI must account for.
* **Findings Assessment:** A comparison of your findings against the original study and your initial hypothesis.
* **Reflections:** A brief reflection on the overall experience using Agentic AI and its utility in real-life research.
